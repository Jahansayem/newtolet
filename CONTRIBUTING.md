# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Keep it simple:

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo or a small bug fix, and say what you plan to do.
2. Fork, make one focused change per PR, and describe what / why / how you tested it.
3. Do not commit secrets, `.env.local` files, or real API keys. Use `.env.local.example`.
4. Do not include other people's phone numbers or personal data in PRs or issues.
5. Keep PRs small and in English or Bangla — either is fine, but keep code comments in English.

By contributing you agree your work is under the MIT licence of this repo.
